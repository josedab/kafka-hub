# Project conduct

Kafka Engineering Hub is a technical learning project. Participation should
make it easier for people with different backgrounds and experience levels to
ask questions, test ideas, and improve the work.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss evidence, code, and operational trade-offs rather than attacking
  people.
- Be patient with incomplete reports and explain specialist terminology.
- Credit sources and other contributors.
- Respect privacy; never post private configuration, credentials, or personal
  information.
- Accept maintainer decisions and correction requests without retaliation.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, sexualized attention, deliberate
humiliation, doxxing, spam, and sustained bad-faith disruption are not
welcome. Technical disagreement is not an excuse for hostile conduct.

## Reporting and enforcement

For a conduct concern, contact the maintainer privately through a contact
method listed on their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/josedab). Do not put
sensitive reports in a public issue. If no safe private channel is available,
use GitHub's abuse-reporting tools.

The maintainer may edit or remove content, issue a warning, limit
participation, or ban an account. Reports will be handled as privately and
consistently as practical, with attention to the safety of affected people.
